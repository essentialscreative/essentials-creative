ABOUT ESSENTIALS CREATIVE

Essentials Creative is a BIPOC-led multimedia collective dedicated to empowering underrepresented artists. Rooted in fluidity, collaboration, and experimentation, we create transformative creative experiences across photography, graphic design, video, projection, animation, and installation.

Based in Austin, San Antonio, and London, our team brings together diverse voices—artists of color, women, and LGBTQIA+ creators—to build inclusive platforms for authentic artistic expression. Our work weaves together unique perspectives, fostering growth, connection, and new narratives in the applied arts.

Join us as we champion creative risk-taking and celebrate diversity in every project, regardless of medium or scale.

HOW WE WORK

Fluidity, diversity, experimentation, and collaboration are the foundational principles that guide all Essentials Creative projects. We begin every engagement by understanding community needs, then apply our multifaceted approach to create comprehensive artistic narratives that serve both artists and audiences.

Rhizomatic Project

A multimedia tapestry installation weaving Indigenous knowledge with scientific perspectives on native plants. Presented at Chicago Botanic Garden's Regenstein Fruit & Vegetable Garden and Austin Central Library's rooftop garden, exploring rhizomes as a metaphor for interconnectedness across biological, cultural, and spiritual dimensions.

Rhizomatic Connections

Exploring networks of knowledge across biological, cultural, and spiritual dimensions through the metaphor of rhizomes - underground stems that grow horizontally and create new connections.

Indigenous Knowledge

Honoring traditional perspectives and wisdom from Indigenous and marginalized communities, centering their understanding of native plants and ecological relationships.

Ecological Interconnectedness

Celebrating the biodiversity and complex relationships within native plant ecosystems, highlighting the importance of environmental stewardship.

Cultural Heritage

Preserving and sharing botanical traditions that connect people to place, history, and ancestral knowledge systems.

Multiple Ways of Knowing

Presenting Indigenous cultural narratives alongside scientific perspectives, demonstrating that different knowledge systems can coexist and enrich our understanding.

Each tapestry explores a specific Texas native plant through both Indigenous cultural narratives and scientific botanical perspectives. The double-sided designs allow viewers to experience both knowledge systems.
