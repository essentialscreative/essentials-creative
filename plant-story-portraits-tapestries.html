Plant Story Portraits · Tapestries

The Tapestries

Ten designs made from the portraits and collages created at our Plant Story Portraits workshop at the Native American Cultural Center. Each one is assembled digitally, printed on fabric, and finished with ribbon embellishments. They first hung at the Austin Powwow on November 21, 2026.

About the work

With permission, we photographed each portrait and collage made at the workshop. Essentials Creative designed the tapestries from those photographs, layering portraits and native plants inside patterned borders, and printed them on fabric. See the full Plant Story Portraits project.
