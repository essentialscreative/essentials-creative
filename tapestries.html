About these works

Each tapestry begins with plant materials sourced ethically from local Indigenous communities and Austin-area gardens. We use traditional dyeing techniques passed down through generations, creating colors that speak to the plants' stories and healing properties. The hand-loom process allows us to weave in botanical patterns that mirror each plant's natural growth patterns and cultural significance.

These works extend our Plant Story Cards research and connect to the botanical themes in our Rhizomatic installations.

Questions before you buy

How long does shipping take?

Pieces ship from Austin within 5–7 business days of purchase. US delivery is typically 3–5 business days after that, fully tracked.

Can I return a tapestry?

Tapestries are accepted for return within 14 days of delivery in original condition. Buyer covers return shipping. Made-to-order and one-of-one pieces are final sale once produced.

How do I care for it?

Spot clean only with cool water and a mild soap. Avoid direct sunlight for prolonged display. We include a care card with every piece.

Can I commission a custom tapestry?

Yes. Custom and large-scale commissions for homes, gardens, and institutional spaces start at $2,400. Tell us about your project.

Do you ship internationally?

Not yet — currently US only. Get in touch if you're abroad and we'll see what's possible case-by-case.
