Stay Wild

An ecological group exhibition curated by Essentials Creative for the Austin Studio Tour.

The Resilience of Nature

Stay Wild is a group exhibition curated by Essentials Creative for the 2024 Austin Studio Tour, presented at Culture Complex / Big Medium on South Congress, November 9–10 and 16–17, 2024.

Nine artists take up the resilience of nature — urban environments, wild spaces, pollinators, and plant life — through painting, video, collage, and immersive light installation.

  • Curated by Essentials Creative; cornerstone event of the Austin Studio Tour
  • With Anahita (Ani) Bradberry, Angela Fox, Essentials Creative, Jasmine Zelaya, Manik Raj Nakra, Natalie Shaw, Paloma Mayorga, Rodell Warner, and Virginia L. Montgomery (VLM)
  • Painting, video, collage, and immersive light installation
  • Free admission with artist talks during exhibition weekends
