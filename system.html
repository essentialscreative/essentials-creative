THE POSITION

Essentials Creative is a stencil-print school in the Austin–San Antonio corridor, in the lineage from kappazuri through Mori Yoshitoshi and Takahashi Hiromitsu, grounded in the heritage of our members. We adopt the constraint, not the imagery. The technique is the school.

AMENDABLE A position statement is not a slogan. It is a thesis we can be argued out of. Members may amend.
SIGNED IN THE WORK The byline is the work itself. No member is named on the artifact; EC is the named anonymous.
VOLATILITY OF "WE" Misa Yamamoto · Fabian Villa · Steven Casanova · Sixto-Juan Zavala · plus collaborators. The "we" rotates without erasing.

vi · ma · iki · notan · mitate
間
Ma
the charged interval
i
Not "negative space." Ma is the active interval. Budget the interval first; size the element to fit it. The empty is load-bearing, not leftover.
粋
Iki
concealed effort · allure
ii
Bitai + ikiji + akirame. Hide the effort. The most expensive detail is the smallest. Never tell the audience you're cool.
濃淡
Notan
dark/light is the composition
iii
Squint at the work. Before color, before type — reduce to 2cm grayscale thumbnail. If the dark/light pattern is weak, nothing will save it.
見立
Mitate
visual substitution · high/low collision
iv
Cast X in the role of Y. Both readings present at once. The viewer's intelligence is the design material. Not parody — parody flattens.

EC · v0.3 禅 · ZEN 07 / 12
則
08 · 8 RULES · OPERATIONAL
viii · what you do · every time · in order
i

Technique first.

Decide the medium before anything else. Riso · screen · cut. The medium decides 80% of what follows.

ii

Pick a cell.

Open the matrix. Month × register. The intersection IS the brief.

iii

Design at viewing size.

Phone thumbnail? A2 wall? Billboard? Set the true size first.

iv

One mon · circle frame.

One motif, large, in a clear field. Need richer? Conjugate (basic / maru-ni / mitsu / chigai).

v

Spark = importance.

Accent color marks the most important zone. Not category. Heat-mapping, not description.

vi

Crop · type at fidelity.

Stop centering. Let figures bleed past. Type shares the drawing's edge — never pasted-over.

vii

Amplify · then reduce.

Keep the misregister, the bleed, the grain. Then: remove one element. Still reads? Remove again.

viii

Number visible. Stop.

Edition mark is part of the design. Commit and finish.

09 · 3 CONTRADICTIONS · THE ENGINE
ix · kept alive — the fights are the design questions
α

Sōsaku-hangavs.Mingei

"The artist owns every step and signs the work." vs. "The craftsman is anonymous and serves utility."

After Serizawa: the collective is named, the members anonymous. EC signs; no individual byline. The named anonymous.
β

Mingeivs.Zen aesthetic

Beauty in everyday utility · rice bowl every meal. vs. Tea-ceremony Zen · rice bowl used once.

Both registers: everyday work = Mingei (IG drops, planting flyers, zines hand-to-hand). Ceremonial work = Zen (installations, the FAE deck, one-off cut paper). Same grammar.
γ

Onchi expressionvs.Seijaku calm

Print as expression — emotional, dynamic. vs. Energized calm — stillness.

Mori's struggle, our brand tone: the subject is urgent (Land Back · Water Back · the monarch refusing the border). The medium is still. Pressure inside calm.

12 · COLOPHON · 奥書
xii · commit · finish · stop

The cap is the cap. The work begins.

This is the broadside. Twelve panels — one for each month, in obedience to the matrix. Every panel a notan-pass kappa-zuri composition, mitate-borrowed from the register of a 1920s Mingei catalogue crossed with an Edo saijiki almanac. The system is the broadside; the broadside is the system.

The grammar is locked at 4 · 7 · 8 · 3 — four operative concepts, seven Zen disciplines, eight procedural rules, three productive contradictions. Hanafuda has been the same deck for one hundred and fifty years. We aim for the same fidelity.

Heritage motifs (Tenango · amate · maque · Sequoyah · Apache coil · Mon · Adinkra · Azulejo · Placa) remain visibly absent. They will appear when member-sourced and consented to, not before. The absence is the system.

The path to presence: a member sketches the mark by hand on paper; the sketch passes through ~/Tools/sketch-to-svg/ for faithful vector translation; the resulting SVG enters the library with the member-chosen attribution line on Panel 11. No member-mark is drawn by my hand. The full four-step protocol — propose · carve · review · enter — is published as protocol.html (the Hanga Protocol), governed by CARE Principles.

The matrix is now a machine: the studio takes a cell × motif × spark × medium and outputs a print-ready composition. The machine-readable token spine lives at tokens.json (DTCG spec). Decisions are recorded in decisions/. History at DESIGN-CHANGELOG.md.
