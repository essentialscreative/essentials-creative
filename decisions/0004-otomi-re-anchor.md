# ADR-0004 · The Otomí re-anchor

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-05-22 (origin) · 2026-05-28 (formalized as ADR)
- **Supersedes:** —
- **Superseded by:** —

## Context

The collective's named Indigenous heritage threads include Otomí, Purépecha, Apache, and Cherokee. The four threads have different states of relationship:

- **Otomí / Purépecha** — grounded center (Tejano lineage; named family connection).
- **Apache** — family oral history, mom's side (held as honored trace pending reconnection).
- **Cherokee** — uncertain lineage (held as honored trace pending reconnection).

A naive approach would treat all four as equally available source-material. That risks claiming what hasn't been earned, particularly with Cherokee Sequoyah syllabary forms — a script that carries enormous Cherokee cultural weight and is widely misused.

## Decision

**Re-anchor mark language on Otomí / Purépecha forms** (Tenango stitch · amate cut · *maque* lacquer · featherwork). Hold **Cherokee Sequoyah-script and Apache forms as HOLD placeholders** until those relationships deepen — visibly absent from the library, never invented from outside.

The Otomí have an active history of refusing appropriation of their patterns. Any Otomí mark in the library must come from a member-sourced reference under the Hanga Protocol — never from stock libraries.

## Consequences

- Panel 11 of the broadside shows the Cherokee and Apache slots as **HOLD** (grayed, dashed, half-opacity) — the absence is the system.
- The Hanga Protocol (Panel 06) explicitly names these as `HOLD` slots with the reasoning included.
- This is the working rule until either (a) lineage reconnection allows leading with these forms, or (b) the collective decides otherwise.

## What this is NOT

- It is not a permanent ban — it is a *current state* tied to current relationships.
- It is not a denial of the lineages — they are named, honored, traced.
- It is not a rule against using these traditions for inspiration in reading and learning — only against producing marks "in the style of" without earned relationship.
