# ADR-0001 · The Four-Step Synthesis

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-05-27
- **Supersedes:** —
- **Superseded by:** —

## Context

By v0.2 the system had absorbed three movements (Sōsaku-hanga · Mingei · Hisamatsu's 7 Zen disciplines) plus four operative concepts (Ma · Iki · Notan · Mitate). Stacked, this is ~19 principles plus contradictions — too many to operate from. Without a stack-order, the principles compete instead of compounding.

## Decision

Adopt a **four-step synthesis** that orders the frameworks as preconditions for each other:

1. **Pick a technique** — Mingei (utility-grade craft, anonymous, useful, cheap, made-in-quantity, selfless).
2. **Own every step** — Sōsaku-hanga (`jiga / jikoku / jizuri` — self-drawn, self-carved, self-printed).
3. **Apply Zen at each step** — Hisamatsu's 7 disciplines + the 4 operative concepts as tests.
4. **Sit in the contradictions** — Serizawa Keisuke's model: the named anonymous, the everyday + ceremonial, the urgent subject in still medium.

Step 4 is non-negotiable: the contradictions are kept alive, not resolved.

## Consequences

- The grammar has a *direction of operation*. A new principle is judged against where it would slot in the stack, not as an addition to a flat list.
- The "anonymous craftsman who signs his work" (Serizawa) is the school's working position.
- Three productive contradictions (α Sōsaku-vs-Mingei, β Mingei-vs-Zen, γ Onchi-vs-Seijaku) are documented in `grammar.html` (v0.2) / Panel 09 (v0.3 broadside) as the engine, not bugs to fix.
