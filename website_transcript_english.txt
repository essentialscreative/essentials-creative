Mission:
- BIPOC-led collective celebrating experimentation, fluidity, diversity & collaboration through multimedia art and cultural storytelling.

RHIZOMATIC PAGE
---------------
Title: Rhizomatic - Essentials Creative

Header:
- Rhizomatic
- A collaborative multimedia art installation exploring cultural and scientific stories of diversity and interconnectedness through Indigenous knowledge and native plants

Exhibition Overview:
- Rhizomatic is a site-responsive multimedia installation that adapts to each venue's architectural and cultural context. The work consists of large-scale double-sided tapestries that create an immersive environment exploring the interconnections between Indigenous knowledge systems and botanical science.
- Adaptable Scale: Flexible installation accommodates spaces from 800-3,000 sq ft, with modular tapestry arrangements
- Site-Specific: Content adapts to local native plant species and Indigenous cultural contexts of each venue

Past Venues & Impact:
- Chicago Botanic Garden (September - November 2024): Exhibited in the Regenstein Center, reaching over 15,000 visitors during the fall season. The installation featured native Illinois prairie plants and incorporated Potawatomi cultural perspectives.
- Austin Central Library (March - May 2025): Installed in the Solar Canopy outdoor gallery space, engaging with Austin's diverse community. Featured Texas native plants and incorporated Coahuiltecan and Apache knowledge systems.

Installation Documentation:
- Chicago Botanic Garden - Regenstein Center (9 Installation Views)
- Austin Central Library - Solar Canopy Gallery (9 Installation Views)

Educational Programming:
- Essentials Creative offers hands-on creative workshops exploring the artistic techniques and cultural contexts behind the Rhizomatic installation. Participants engage with Indigenous knowledge systems through collaborative art-making processes that honor traditional perspectives while exploring contemporary artistic expression.
- Recent Workshops: Portrait & Collage Workshop - Austin Central Library • October 2025 • Community engagement through multimedia art-making

About Essentials Creative:
- Essentials Creative is a multimedia collective producing holistic applied arts experiences. Our multifaceted approach incorporates photography, graphic design, video, projection, animation, and installation to create comprehensive artistic narratives. We create platforms for underrepresented artists to express themselves and find support within their field. Our collective is composed of and embraces artists of color, women, and members of the LGBTQIA+ community. Fluidity, diversity, experimentation, and collaboration are the foundational principles that guide all Essentials Creative projects, regardless of medium or scale.
