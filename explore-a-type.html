Land Back. Water Back. Data Back. Three literal cycles of return — not a picture of one.

01

back more

Every technology in the room is held to the same test the visitor is. If it doesn't, it doesn't belong in the room.

02

The threshold · you give first

You enter
by giving

A handful of soil. A word. A pour of water. In return you're handed an anonymous token — no name, no email, no trace.

03

Movement one

Land
Back

Press the soil into a living bed. A small, bodily act of return to the ground — kneeling, pressing, leaving a mark the next visitor builds on.

04

Movement two

Water
Back

Pour your water into the reeds. It moves through a living phytoremediation loop and comes back measurably clearer. The cleaning is real ecology — not a video of cleaning.

05

Movement three · the core test

Data Back

Give a word. Watch it join a projection field woven with others'. Then choose:

  • Take it— print it, carry it out
  • Hear it— read back into the room
  • Gift it— the only path to retention
  • Erase it— deleted on screen, in front of you

Here, deletion is the norm — the opposite of every other screen. Retention is the exception you choose.

06

You leave
receiving

The loop the threshold opened closes. You gave first; the room gave back.
