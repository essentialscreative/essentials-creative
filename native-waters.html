Floating Projection

Native Waters

A floating projection of monarchs and Indigenous motifs over Lady Bird Lake, Austin.

Light on the Water

Native Waters is a projection work staged on the water of Lady Bird Lake in downtown Austin. A floating screen carries a monarch butterfly and Indigenous motifs framed by a vivid textile-inspired border, its colors doubling in the lake's reflection as paddlers drift past beneath the skyline.

The piece brings Indigenous iconography back to a waterway that has always been a gathering place, setting the slow movement of wings and water against the city's vertical glass and light.

  • A floating screen anchored on Lady Bird Lake, viewed from the water and the trail
  • Monarch and Indigenous motifs inside a textile-inspired border
  • The projection doubles in the lake's reflection
