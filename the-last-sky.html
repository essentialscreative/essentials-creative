The Last Sky

The Land of Spirit Waters banners at the eighth Texas Biennial — Blaffer Art Museum, Houston.

Texas Biennial 2024

Essentials Creative was selected through open call for The Last Sky, the eighth edition of the Texas Biennial, at Blaffer Art Museum, University of Houston — on view September 27, 2024 through March 9, 2025.

The banners of the Land of Spirit Waters series, first hung in the trees at Yanaguana Garden, moved indoors for the Biennial — moon panther, sun bird, and spirit figures on the museum's white walls.

Established in 2005, the Texas Biennial is the longest-running state biennial in the United States. The 2024 edition was curated by Erika Mei Chua Holum, Ashley Dehoyos Sauder, and Coka Treviño, alongside artists including Anahita Bradberry, Jessica Carolina González, Guadalupe Hernandez, Antonio Lechuga Jr., Gabriel Martinez, and Gilbert Rocha. Installation photography by Francisco Ramos.
