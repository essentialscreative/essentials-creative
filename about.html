About Essentials Creative

Site-responsive multimedia studio in Austin

We make art with place and community

Essentials Creative is a multimedia collective creating installations, projections, and experiences that respond to specific sites and their stories. We work at the intersection of cultural memory and contemporary expression.

How We Work

Every project begins with listening — to the land, the community, and the histories embedded in each space. We collaborate with cultural organizations, botanical gardens, and community spaces to create work that honors Indigenous, Latinx, and Asian knowledge systems while embracing new forms of storytelling.

Our practice is rhizomatic: non-hierarchical, interconnected, and regenerative.

What We Make

  • Installations — Site-specific works that transform spaces
  • Projections — Large-scale video mapped onto architecture and landscapes
  • Photography — Documentary and artistic photography for cultural archives
  • Design — Visual identity and graphics for community organizations
  • Video — Films that preserve and share cultural narratives

Working With Us

We're available for commissions, exhibitions, and partnerships. We prioritize projects that center underrepresented voices and create platforms for cultural exchange.
