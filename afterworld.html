Afterworld

Exploring afterlife traditions and multicultural remembrance through immersive installation.

Exploring Afterlife Traditions

Afterworld is a multimedia installation that explores afterlife traditions and multiculturalism, including Day of the Dead, Obon, and other cultural practices surrounding death and remembrance.

This immersive experience weaves together diverse cultural narratives about the afterlife, creating a space for reflection on mortality, memory, and the various ways communities honor their ancestors.

Displayed at Presa House, San Antonio and Asian American Resource Center, Austin, TX
