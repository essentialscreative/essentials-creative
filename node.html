About the Program

Node, also known as Video Art Night, is an open-format projection program operating across Austin. Each iteration finds a new site and listens to it: a wall, a courtyard, a window, an industrial bay. The program brings together BIPOC artists, community organizations, and cultural institutions through screenings, guerrilla projections, and site-specific projection commissions.

  • Open-format screenings welcoming BIPOC artists
  • Guerrilla projections in unexpected sites across Austin
  • Site-specific projection commissions for venues and organizations
  • Collaborations with BIPOC-led partners and cultural institutions
  • Each iteration shaped by the architecture and community of its site
