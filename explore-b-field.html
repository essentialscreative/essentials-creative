◀ B · Living Field

Essentials Creative · a reciprocity installation

Give Back

A living, centerless network — soil, water, and data, each given and returned. Scroll to move through the room.

The threshold · you give first

You enter by giving

A handful of soil. A word. A pour of water. In return: an anonymous token — no name, no email, no trace.

Movement one

Land Back

Press the soil into a living bed. A small, bodily act of return to the ground — leaving a mark the next visitor builds on.

Movement two

Water Back

Pour your water into the reeds. It moves through a living loop and comes back measurably clearer. The cleaning is real ecology — not a video of cleaning.

Movement three · the core test

Data Back

Give a word. Watch it join a field woven with others'. Then choose — and watch some words erased, on screen, in front of you:

  • Take it
  • Hear it
  • Gift it
  • Erase it

Deletion is the norm here. Retention is the exception you choose.

You leave receiving

The loop the threshold opened closes. You gave first; the room gave back.

All of this sits on the I-35 corridor — one of the fastest-growing data-center markets in the country, consuming the same land, water, power, and data the work returns. See the figures →
